# RoadMesh terms of use

*Draft for legal review under Cambodian law (AUDIT L-20).*

1. **Driver assistance, not a guarantee.** RoadMesh warns about vehicles whose drivers also run RoadMesh.
   - It cannot detect vehicles, people or obstacles without the app.
   - Its warnings can be late, missing or wrong (radio range, GPS error, phones switched off).
   - It never brakes, steers or controls the vehicle.
   - You remain fully responsible for driving safely and lawfully.
2. **Do not handle the phone while driving.** Set up before you drive. While moving, glance at the screen only, and use a phone holder or CarPlay.
3. **Fair use.** Do not send false messages, interfere with the radio messages of others, or try to identify other users. The app revokes devices that misbehave.

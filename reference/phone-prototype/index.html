Nearest vehicles
Every vehicle on the map is simulated in your browser. Each one runs the RoadMesh app: it broadcasts its position and speed over a short-range peer-to-peer radio, relays messages for others (multi-hop mesh), and scores the driving behavior it observes. Tap any vehicle to inspect it.

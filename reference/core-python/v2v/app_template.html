      What runs inside each car, every 100 ms
      1. Build a message: position, speed, heading, turn rate and braking, 10 times a second.
      2. Sign it with a temporary certificate that changes every 5 minutes, so the car can't be tracked.
      3. Broadcast to every car within 300 m. Buildings block eyes, not radio.
      4. Relay: forward each new, verified message once to cars further away, up to 8 hops, so a warning can travel kilometres down the road. The original signature travels with it, so a relay can pass a message on but can't change it.
      5. Check what arrives: drop forged or revoked senders, impossible speeds, old or future timestamps and position jumps. Report the sender.
      6. Predict where every nearby car will be over the next 5 s and look for overlapping footprints.
      7. Decide: apply right of way, choose the gentlest braking that leaves a 1 s gap, and tell the driver.
